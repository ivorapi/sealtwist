RapiTwist 1.5.0 for WoW Forever (interface 16001)

Left-click the minimap button or type /rapitwist to open settings.
Click First or Second's seal name to cycle through the seals. Picking the
other slot's seal exchanges them. Click its echo goal to cycle from 1 to 3.
Choose Apply and reload UI outside combat to save the pair and activate it.
Closing the panel discards unapplied seal changes.

Gold arcs track First and blue arcs track Second. Both sides fill together.
At the goal, the arcs briefly pulse in the destination color and "Swap to"
appears below the character with the other seal's name. Only the two selected seals appear. Command (3) and Righteousness
(1) are the defaults. Fury, Justice, Light and Wisdom also support echo goals.
Crusader and Martyrdom display their active name with a manual timing label.
A seal that your character has not learned can be selected for later use.

Right-click the minimap button outside combat and drag the tracker to move it.
Drag the minimap button itself to reposition the button.
Use Size and Opacity to adjust the display.

/st or /rapitwist       Open settings.
/st unlock              Move the tracker outside combat.
/st lock                Finish positioning.
/st scale 1.2           Set scale (0.6 to 2).
/st combat              Show only in combat with a selected seal active.
/st always              Also show outside combat with a selected seal active.
/st hide or /st show    Hide or show the tracker.
/st reset               Restore appearance, visibility and position defaults.
/st debug               Print version and tracker status.
