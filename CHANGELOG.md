# Changelog

## 1.6.3
Use a subdued slate outline for empty arcs. Keep HIT and SWAP on separate baselines and draw the coral ready state above filled segments.

## 1.6.2
Restore HIT and the progress arcs before the first echo and after each swap by removing the looping wait-layer animation.

## 1.6.1
Remove the solid SWAP badge and align the arc pulse with the underlying stroke. The ready cue hides the waiting layer.

## 1.6.0
Show HIT while waiting for echoes. At the selected goal, both arcs pulse coral and SWAP appears with the destination seal.

## 1.5.1
Antialiased arc textures replace joined line segments. Empty arcs retain the active slot color after a swap.

## 1.5.0
Side arcs now flank the character. Both arcs pulse in the destination color at the selected echo goal. The layout recenters once on upgrade and can be moved from the minimap.

## 1.4.0
Choose the seal pair and echo goal for each slot. Added English swap cues, settings and documentation, plus active-state tracking for seals without a mapped echo.

## 1.3.0
Renamed the addon and distribution to RapiTwist and added the /rapitwist command.

## 1.2.3
Added the Rapi author credit.

## 1.2.2
Changed the second and third rune thresholds to exact stack counts and tested duplicate first-echo updates.

## 1.2.1
Added rune glow and transparent swap cues.

## 1.2.0
Introduced the rune display and a dedicated minimap tooltip.

## 1.1.0
Added the compact tracker, minimap controls and appearance settings. Removed demo mode.

## 1.0.0
Added native Command and Righteousness echo tracking.
